## 4. Content and Copyright

Nest Music does not host, store, or distribute copyrighted music content. It functions as a client/tool that interacts with third-party services or local files, depending on its implementation.

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of Nest Music complies with:

- The terms of service of any third-party service it connects to.
- Applicable copyright laws in their jurisdiction.

The maintainers of Nest Music do not condone piracy or copyright infringement.

## 5. Privacy

Nest Music does not knowingly collect personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for the app to function, unless explicitly stated otherwise in a dedicated `PRIVACY.md` file (if present in this repository).

No user data is sold to third parties.

If the app integrates with external APIs or services, users should also review the privacy policies of those third-party services.

## 6. Security

If you discover a security vulnerability in Nest Music, please **do not open a public GitHub issue**. Instead, report it privately by contacting the maintainer directly through GitHub.

We aim to acknowledge security reports as soon as possible and address confirmed vulnerabilities in a timely manner.

## 7. Community Conduct

The Nest Music community (including GitHub Issues, Discussions, and the official subreddit r/nestmusic) is governed by the following principles:

- Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are not tolerated.
- Keep discussions relevant to Nest Music.
- No spam, unauthorized advertising, or referral links.
- Do not impersonate maintainers or distribute unofficial builds as if they were official.
- Do not share sensitive information (passwords, tokens, API keys, personal data) in issues, posts, or logs.

Violations may result in removal of content, temporary or permanent bans from community spaces, at the discretion of the maintainers/moderators.
